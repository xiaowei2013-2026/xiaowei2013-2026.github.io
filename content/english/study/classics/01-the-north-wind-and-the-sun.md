## English

The North Wind and the Sun once argued about which of them was stronger. As they argued, they saw a traveler walking along the road in a warm coat. They agreed that whoever could make the traveler take off his coat would be the stronger one.

The North Wind blew as hard as he could, but the harder he blew, the more tightly the traveler held his coat. At last, the Wind gave up. Then the Sun shone gently. Soon the traveler felt warm and took off his coat.

Gentleness can be more powerful than force.

## 中文大意

北风和太阳争论谁更强大，于是比赛看谁能让路人脱下外套。北风越猛烈，路人把衣服裹得越紧；太阳温和地照耀，路人很快便主动脱下了外套。温和有时比强迫更有力量。

## 必记表达

- **argue about...**：争论……
- **as hard as he could**：尽他所能地猛烈
- **the harder..., the more...**：越……，越……
- **give up**：放弃
- **more powerful than**：比……更有力量

## 背诵提示

按“争论—比赛—北风失败—太阳成功—寓意”五个画面记忆。重点掌握 `the harder..., the more...` 句型。
