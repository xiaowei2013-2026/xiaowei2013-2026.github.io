## English

One day, a lion was sleeping in the forest when a little mouse ran across his paw and woke him. The angry lion caught the mouse and was about to eat him.

“Please let me go,” cried the mouse. “Perhaps I can help you someday.” The lion laughed at the idea, but he set the mouse free.

Some days later, the lion was caught in a hunter's net. He roared for help. The mouse heard him, ran to the net, and bit through the ropes with his sharp teeth. Soon the lion was free.

No act of kindness is ever wasted, and even the smallest friend can be a great help.

## 中文大意

狮子放过了一只小老鼠。后来狮子被猎网困住，小老鼠咬断绳索救出了它。善意不会白费，再弱小的朋友也可能带来很大的帮助。

## 必记表达

- **be about to do**：正要做某事
- **let me go**：放我走
- **set...free**：释放……
- **be caught in**：被困在……中
- **bite through**：咬穿；咬断
- **an act of kindness**：善举

## 背诵提示

先用过去时复述四步情节：`caught the mouse → set it free → was caught in a net → was saved by the mouse`。
