### 感谢与歉意
感谢：Thank you!/Thanks

非常感谢：Thank you very much!/Thanks a lot!

谢谢你的帮助：Thank you/Thanks for your help!

你真是太好了：It's very nice/kind of you!

还是感谢你（别人未能帮上忙时）：Thank you all the same.

**如何回应感谢**

You are welcome，不用谢

That's all right./That's OK.不客气

It's my pleasure.这是我的荣幸

My pleasure.我的荣幸

I am glad you like it. 我很高兴你喜欢它。一般是送礼物中会使用。

**如何用英语表达歉意**

Sorry/I am sorry/I am very sorry

I am sorry about it!/I am sorry to hear that!听到别人不幸的时候

Excuse me用来打断/打扰别人。Excuse me. May I come in?打扰一下，我能进来吗？

**如何用英语回应抱歉**

**That's all right/That's OK.没关系**，万能句

It doesn't matter.不碍事

Never mind.别介意。

That's nothing./Nothing.没什么
