## The Power of Dream · 梦想的力量

背诵日期：2026-10-06

### 英文原文

Dreams are the fuel that keeps our life moving forward. Everyone has dreams. Some dreams are small, while others are grand. A dream may not come true overnight. It requires patience, effort and courage. When we meet difficulties, our dreams remind us why we started. We may fail many times, but we should never give up. Hold fast to your dreams, for if dreams die, life is like a broken-winged bird that cannot fly.

### 中文参考

梦想是推动我们生活不断前进的燃料。每个人都有梦想。有些梦想很小，有些则很宏大。梦想未必会一夜之间实现，它需要耐心、努力和勇气。当我们遇到困难时，梦想会提醒我们为什么出发。我们可能失败很多次，但不应轻言放弃。紧紧抓住你的梦想，因为如果梦想消逝，生命就像一只折断翅膀、无法飞翔的鸟。

### 背诵分段

1. 从 “Dreams are the fuel…” 到 “…others are grand.”：梦想与动力。
2. 从 “A dream may not…” 到 “…patience, effort and courage.”：实现梦想需要什么。
3. 从 “When we meet…” 到 “…never give up.”：遇到困难时的坚持。
4. 最后一句 “Hold fast…”：梦想与折翼鸟的比喻。
